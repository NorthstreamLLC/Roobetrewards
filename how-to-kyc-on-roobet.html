Does Roobet Require ID? Roobet KYC Explained

What Roobet asks for, when it asks, how long review takes and how to pass verification first try — checked against Roobet's official Help Center.

Does Roobet Require ID?

Yes. Roobet's Level 2 verification requires a passport, driving licence or other government-issued ID, uploaded as a front-and-back image in your account settings. You can sign up, deposit and play first, but expect ID to be requested before withdrawals and some account features — so verify early.

KYC (“Know Your Customer”) is the identity check licensed casinos run to prevent fraud and protect accounts. Source: Roobet's official Level 2 Verification article. For a full illustrated walkthrough, our sister site HowToKYC.com covers every screen.

When Does Roobet Make You Verify?

Roobet does not publish a fixed dollar amount that triggers verification. Its compliance team can request it at any time, and in practice it usually comes up at one of these points:

  • When you request a withdrawal, especially your first or a large one
  • When you want to use features that need Level 2, such as receiving tips
  • During a routine or manual account review

You will see threshold figures quoted on other sites. None of them come from Roobet. The only reliable way to never be held up is to verify the day you sign up.

How Long Does Roobet Verification Take?

There is no guaranteed turnaround. Clean uploads with matching details move fastest; anything that triggers a manual review takes longer. What slows it down and when to chase support: Roobet KYC verification time.

Pass It First Try

Three mistakes cause almost every rejection: blurry or cropped photos, account details that don't exactly match the ID (name spelling, date of birth), and expired documents. Get those right and it's one-and-done.

Roobet KYC FAQ

Does Roobet require ID?
Yes, for full account access. Roobet's Level 2 verification asks for a passport, driving licence or other government-issued ID, uploaded as a front-and-back image in your account settings. You can sign up and deposit first, but expect ID to be requested before withdrawals and some account features.
At what amount does Roobet make you verify?
Roobet does not publish a fixed dollar threshold. Its compliance team can request verification at any time, and it is most often triggered by a withdrawal or account review. Anyone quoting an exact number is guessing — verifying early is the only reliable way to avoid a delay.
Can you use Roobet without verifying your identity?
You can register, deposit and play before completing Level 2, but some features stay limited until you verify, and an unverified account can be held up the moment you try to cash out.
Which ID documents does Roobet accept?
Roobet's official Level 2 article lists three: a passport, a driving licence, or a government-issued identification document. Upload a clear front-and-back image of the original — not a photocopy — through roobet.com account settings.
How long does Roobet verification take?
There is no guaranteed time. Roobet says its compliance team reviews documents daily and will contact you after manual review if anything else is needed. Clear photos and details that match your ID exactly are what keep it quick.
My Roobet KYC got rejected — what now?
Re-upload a sharp, glare-free image of the original document with all four corners visible, and make sure your account name and date of birth match it exactly. If it is still rejected, contact Roobet's official Help Center. Never send ID images to anyone over Telegram, Discord or email.
