Roobet Withdrawal Guide: Times, Minimums & Rules

You hit the win — now get it out clean. The exact rules Roobet applies before a cash-out, how long each coin takes, and what to fix when a withdrawal won't go through. Checked against Roobet's official Help Center.

How to Withdraw from Roobet (Quick Answer)

Wager 100% of your deposit, wait for the deposit's blockchain confirmations, then open Cashier → Withdraw, pick your coin, paste your own wallet's receive address, enter the amount and select Withdraw Funds. Roobet charges no withdrawal fee — you only pay the network fee shown before you confirm.

Roobet Withdrawal Requirements

🔁

Wager 100% of Your Deposit

Roobet's AML rule: a crypto or cash deposit must be wagered in full before withdrawal. Deposit $200, wager $200. Only settled sports bets count.

⛓️

Deposit Confirmed

Your deposit needs 1 confirmation (BTC, LTC, XRP) or 3 (ETH, USDT, USDC) before those funds can be withdrawn or tipped.

🪪

Account in Good Standing

Compliance can request ID verification at any time — a withdrawal is the most common trigger.

The full breakdown, including the 30% rule on received tips, is in our Roobet wagering requirements guide. Verify early with our KYC guide or HowToKYC.com so a win never waits on documents.

Withdrawing, Step by Step

Step 1

In your external wallet, open the coin you want to receive and copy its Receive / Deposit address.

Step 2

On Roobet, open Cashier → Withdraw and pick the same coin. Litecoin and Solana are fast with small network fees; Bitcoin is slower and pricier.

Step 3

Paste the address and triple-check the network. Sending to the wrong chain can permanently lose funds, and Roobet does not process withdrawals to smart-contract addresses.

Step 4

Enter the amount (at or above that coin's minimum), check the network fee, and select Withdraw Funds.

How Long Do Roobet Withdrawals Take?

Once the withdrawal is sent, timing is down to the blockchain. Roobet's Help Center lists these confirmation requirements before your wallet receives the funds — some wallets ask for more:

To track a withdrawal, grab the transaction ID from your Roobet notifications or withdrawal history and paste it into that coin's block explorer.

Minimum Withdrawal & Fees

Roobet doesn't publish one fixed minimum withdrawal — it varies by coin, and the live minimum and network fee are shown on the Withdraw tab before you confirm. Roobet itself charges no withdrawal fee; the only cost is the blockchain network fee. If you're below the minimum on one coin, a cheaper chain like Litecoin is usually the fix.

If Your Withdrawal Won't Go Through

🔁

Wager Not Met

You haven't wagered 100% of the deposit yet. Check your remaining requirement in the Cashier.

⏳

Deposit Unconfirmed

The deposit is still waiting on its 1 or 3 blockchain confirmations.

📉

Below Minimum

Each coin has its own minimum — switch coins or top the amount up.

📄

Contract Address

Roobet can't send to smart-contract addresses. Use a standard wallet address.

🪪

Verification Open

Compliance has requested documents. Complete KYC and resubmit.

🌍

Region Restricted

Roobet doesn't serve every country — see the restricted countries list.

Roobet Withdrawal FAQ

How long do Roobet withdrawals take?
Once your requirements are met, the withdrawal is sent and the clock is the blockchain. Roobet's Help Center says a transaction needs 1 confirmation for BTC, LTC, XRP, TRX and DOGE, or 3 for ETH, USDT and USDC, before your wallet credits it. Some wallets require more confirmations, and Bitcoin slows down when the network is congested.
What is the minimum wager to withdraw crypto on Roobet?
100% of your deposit. Under Roobet's AML policy you must wager the full amount of a crypto deposit before you can withdraw, tip or use instant raffles. Deposit $200 and you need $200 in wagers first. Only settled sportsbook bets count.
What is the minimum withdrawal on Roobet?
It depends on the coin. Roobet does not publish one fixed minimum; the current minimum and network fee for each currency are shown on the Withdraw tab of the Cashier before you confirm. If your balance is below it, switch to a different coin or keep playing until you clear it.
Can you cash out on Roobet?
Yes. Roobet pays out in crypto to your own external wallet: BTC, ETH, LTC, USDT, USDC, XRP, TRX, DOGE, SUI, SOL and BNB. Copy the receive address from your wallet, paste it into the Cashier's Withdraw tab, enter the amount and select Withdraw Funds.
Why can't I withdraw from Roobet?
Usually one of five things: you haven't wagered 100% of your deposit yet, the deposit hasn't reached its required confirmations, the amount is below that coin's minimum, the address is a smart contract (Roobet doesn't support those), or your account has a verification or compliance request open. Check each in that order before contacting support.
Does Roobet verify you before a withdrawal?
It can. Roobet's compliance team may request Level 2 ID verification at any time, and a withdrawal is the most common trigger. Verifying the day you sign up means a win is never left waiting on documents.
Can I withdraw a tip someone sent me on Roobet?
Yes, once it is wagered. Roobet's AML guidance says a received tip must be wagered at 30% of its value before it can be withdrawn — a $100 tip needs $30 in wagers. Our tips guide covers when tipping unlocks on an account.
Are there withdrawal fees on Roobet?
Roobet doesn't charge a withdrawal fee of its own. You do pay the blockchain network fee, which is shown at the bottom of the Cashier's Withdraw page before you confirm. Faster, cheaper chains like Litecoin or Solana usually cost a fraction of Bitcoin.
