Roobet Wagering Requirements Explained

Depositing, completing KYC and becoming eligible to withdraw are separate steps. This guide explains the current wagering rules for deposits, tips, sportsbook bets and instant raffles.

By Roobet Casino Rewards Editorial Team · Reviewed 7 October 2026 · 8 min read

The short answer

Roobet's current AML guidance says cryptocurrency and cash deposits must be wagered at 100% of the deposited amount before withdrawal. Received tips carry a separate 30% wagering requirement. Only settled sportsbook bets count.

Current Roobet Wagering Rules

₿

Cryptocurrency deposits

Wager 100% of the deposit before withdrawing, sending tips or using instant raffles.

💵

Cash deposits

Wager 100% before withdrawal. Roobet's guidance says tipping is not available from cash deposits.

⚽

Sportsbook wagers

Only settled bets count toward the requirement. A pending bet has not completed the wagering step.

💸

Received tips

Wager 30% of the received tip amount before it becomes eligible for withdrawal.

What “Wager 100%” Means

If you deposit the equivalent of $100 in cryptocurrency, the published rule requires $100 in qualifying wagers before withdrawal eligibility. It does not mean you must lose $100, and it does not promise that every type of wager contributes identically. Check the progress and account messages shown by Roobet rather than estimating from your balance alone.

A wagering requirement is an anti-money-laundering control, not a bonus multiplier. It is also different from the qualification terms for our free-spins offer, leaderboard or wager milestones.

KYC and Wagering Are Different Checks

Completing identity verification does not automatically clear a deposit or tip wagering requirement. Likewise, finishing the wagering amount does not guarantee that an account with an unresolved verification or compliance request can withdraw. Complete the KYC process, check the exact account status and follow any official request shown in your account.

Avoid workarounds

Do not open another account, send funds through another person, use offsetting bets or attempt to disguise fund movement. Those actions can create additional compliance problems. If the displayed progress looks wrong, contact official support.

Why a Withdrawal May Still Be Unavailable

  • The deposit wagering meter has not reached the required amount.
  • A sportsbook wager is still open and therefore does not count as settled.
  • A received tip still has part of its 30% requirement outstanding.
  • Identity verification or another compliance review is incomplete.
  • The selected asset, network or withdrawal details need correction.

Work through the account message first, then use our Roobet withdrawal guide. Never post wallet credentials, authentication codes or identity documents in community chat.

Primary Source and Review Method

The percentages and eligibility statements above were checked against Roobet's official Wagering Requirements article on 7 October 2026. Requirements can change, so the authenticated account screen and official Help Center take priority over this independent guide.
